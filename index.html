The Strategy

① MSNR — Malaysian SNR

Levels are drawn from candle bodies (open/close), never wicks. Wicks are noise; bodies show where price settled.

  • Fresh levels — untouched since creation. First touch gives the strongest reaction; after 2–3 tests the level weakens.
  • Gap SNR — a body-to-body gap is the strongest level type. Price returns to fill it, then rejects hard.
  • SNR Flip — support broken with a body close becomes resistance (and vice versa). The retest of the flip is the classic entry.
  • A / V shapes — sharp reversal candles mark institutional levels.
  • Timeframes — mark on H4/H1, execute on M15/M5.
SL normally 20–40 pips (volatility-adaptive, max 100) · TP1 50 pips · runners to 150–200 pips

② Advanced Price Action

  • Structure — HH/HL = uptrend, LH/LL = downtrend. BOS continues the trend; CHoCH warns of reversal.
  • Liquidity sweeps — gold is the king of stop hunts. Equal highs/lows get swept, then price reverses. Enter after the sweep into your level.
  • Order blocks — last opposite candle before an impulse. On a fresh MSNR level = A+ confluence.
  • Fair Value Gaps — 3-candle imbalances gold loves to fill; use as entry refinement.
  • Sessions — Asia ranges, London fakes, New York delivers.
SL normally 50–80 pips (max 100) · TP1 at 1:1 · runners to 150–200+ pips

③ Execution & Risk Rules

  • Every signal: 1 SL, 3 TPs. Scale out at TP1, move SL to breakeven, let TP2/TP3 run.
  • Minimum 1:2 reward-to-risk on the full position.
  • Risk 1–2% max per trade — gold's volatility punishes oversizing.
  • No chasing: price must come to the level.
  • Stay flat through NFP, CPI, FOMC — trade the post-news retest instead.
  • Avoid the rollover dead zone (spreads widen massively).
Combined model: H4 bias → fresh MSNR level → sweep → M15 confirmation → execute
